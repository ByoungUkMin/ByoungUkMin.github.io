About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Changhee Jung in the CompArch group. My research lies at the intersection of computer architecture and compilers, with a focus on intermittent computing for battery-less energy-harvesting systems. I work on making GPUs and nonvolatile processors practical under frequent power failures: enabling intermittent AI inference on GPUs, equipping nonvolatile processors with volatile caches, and building simulation infrastructure that models power failures, capacitor dynamics, and checkpoint/restart behavior.

Before Purdue, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Bernd Burgstaller, where I worked on persistent lock-free data structures for non-volatile memory and non-blocking synchronization under relaxed memory consistency models. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Sungkyunkwan University.
